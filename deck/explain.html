Bio-Rewards

A referee for AI in biology.

In plain words, for a two minute read.

What it is

An AI suggests an answer. Our tool checks it against real science and gives it a score from 0 to 1. Good answers score high. Made-up answers score zero.

Why it matters

AI invents things with total confidence. That is wonderful for brainstorming and dangerous for real work, because in biology someone runs the experiment and it costs months and money. Our tool is the truth-check that sits between the AI and the lab.

The demo (the climbing line)

A line climbs to 1.000. The AI keeps trying, the referee keeps scoring, and the answer gets better until it is right. A good answer makes the line go up. A fake one stays at zero.
