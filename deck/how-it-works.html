Bio-Rewards · how it works

One math. Four sciences.

The primers, the proteins, the metabolic engineering, the citations. All the same idea underneath.

1. The idea

An AI proposes a biology answer. A reward function scores it from 0 to 1. High means real. Zero means impossible or fake. That score is a fixed calculation, not another opinion, so it does not hallucinate.

2. Every reward is a landscape

3. A good answer makes the search climb

An AI searches this landscape, proposing answers and keeping the best one found so far. Because it only ever keeps the best, the score can only go up, and it is capped at 1. So the search always settles. Where it settles is the verdict. Settling near 1 means it found a real answer. Stalling low means the AI was exploring the wrong place. That is the deterministic layer: truth converges high, fiction gets stuck at zero.

4. What each one actually measures

PrimersMelting temperature from nearest-neighbor thermodynamics. Textbook physics, computed exactly.
Protein binderDevelopability of the sequence: charge, water-hating patches, protease sites, cysteine pairing.
Metabolic (flux balance)Solves the cell's chemistry as an equation system to find how much product a knockout plan yields.
CitationDoes the reference resolve, does the number appear in it, is the database entity really what was claimed.

same skeleton every time: R(x) = [passes hard gates] x weighted sum of calibrated measurements.
